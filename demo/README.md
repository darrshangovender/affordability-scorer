# Demo data (synthetic)

Everything under `demo/data/` is generated by `demo/generate.py` (seeded, reproducible; a test
regenerates it and checks byte equality). No statement, payslip, person, employer account or
outcome here is real, and none comes from LeasEase users or any client.

- 12 personas (`p01`-`p12`) and 2 tampered variants (`t01`, `t02`), each with a bank statement
  in one of four layouts, a payslip and per-line category labels.
- `manifest.json` records each persona's proposed rent, the 12-month outcome label (written by
  the author into the generator, not observed) and the tamper kind.

Personal-information-shaped values exist only so the redactor has something to redact. ID
numbers pass the format and Luhn checks but carry a 30 or 31 February birth date, so they
cannot be anyone's ID. Bank names describe layouts ("FNB-style"), not exports from those banks.
